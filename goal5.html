Goal 5

Communicate, Collaborate & Advocate

Effectively communicate and collaborate to deliver, market, and advocate for library and information services.

Outcome 5a

Apply marketing and/or advocacy principles to demonstrate and promote the value of libraries and information agencies.

Artifact

Design Thinking Project

A design thinking proposal developed for LIS 707 imagining a new public programming initiative at the Huntington Library in San Marino, California.

LIS 707  ·  Design Thinking Project  ·  Amanda Masis ↓ Download PDF
Explanation

The Design Thinking Project demonstrates Outcome 5a by proposing a public programming initiative at the Huntington Library designed to expand the institution's reach and demonstrate its value to new professional communities. The proposal draws on marketing and advocacy frameworks learned in LIS 707, making a structured case for why the library should invest in outreach to urban planning and architecture professionals. It reflects an understanding of how libraries can promote their collections and services strategically to build new audiences and justify institutional investment.

Outcome 5b

Demonstrate effective professional communication to achieve common understanding as an individual or in group settings.

Artifact

Experimental Film Digital Curation Project

A presentation from the digital curation course centered around curating born digital objects across a wide variety of formats. The video is a walkthrough of the curatorial process and objects.

Explanation

The Experimental Film Digital Curation presentation demonstrates Outcome 5b by communicating a curatorial process and explaining experimental film to an audience in a clear and structured way. The walkthrough video talks through technical decisions about metadata, format, and organization, demonstrating the ability to communicate professional work effectively. It reflects the kind of collaborative communication skills needed to work across departments and with non-specialist stakeholders in a library or archive setting.

Outcome 5c

Demonstrate leadership principles in an educational, professional, or community context.

Artifact

CyberNavigator Reflection

A reflection on working as a CyberNavigator at the Chicago Public Library.

CyberNavigator Reflection  ·  Amanda Masis ↓ Download PDF
Explanation

The CyberNavigator Reflection demonstrates Outcome 5c by documenting situations in which I was required to exercise leadership and independent judgment in the absence of formal supervision or institutional support. Working as an independently contracted employee embedded in a community hub, I regularly faced situations that required me to set priorities, make ethical decisions, and take initiative, including navigating complex patron needs around housing, immigration, and legal documents. The reflection shows how this experience shaped my understanding of what leadership looks like in a public-facing library context, and the responsibility that comes with being a trusted presence in a community.
