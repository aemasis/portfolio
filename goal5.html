Goal 5

Communicate, Collaborate & Advocate

Effectively communicate and collaborate to deliver, market, and advocate for library and information services.

Outcome 5a

Apply marketing and/or advocacy principles to demonstrate and promote the value of libraries and information agencies.

Artifact

Design Thinking Project

A design thinking proposal developed for LIS 707 imagining a new public programming initiative at the Huntington Library in San Marino, California. The project proposes a structured presentation series giving urban planning, architecture, and landscape architecture students and professionals hands-on access to the Huntington's architecture and photography collection.

Experimental Film Digital Curation Project

A presentation from the digital curation course centered around curating born digital objects across a wide variety of formats. The organizing theme was experimental film — collecting short films, interviews, images, and text documents related to experimental films and filmmakers. The video is a walkthrough of the curatorial process and objects.
