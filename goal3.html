Goal 3

Navigate, Curate & Create

Navigate, curate and create information across the spectrum of human records.

Outcome 3a

Design resources or tools that facilitate access to information.

Artifact

A Guide to Knitting

A website developed for INF 253, Intro to Web Design. The site is designed to be a resource for people who are just getting started with knitting and want to know more about its historical and cultural significance.

↗ Visit Site
Explanation

A Guide to Knitting is a website I designed for my Intro to Web Design course. It acts as a reference for those just starting to knit or who are interested in learning more about the craft. I designed the website to provide clear access to different resources relating to knitting including terminology, history, books, and examples of knits from around the world.

Outcome 3b

Utilize resources or tools to manage information.

Artifact

The Whole Sky On A Page

A website built for the History of the Book course, thematized around a mock exhibition on the history of astronomy told from the perspective of material book culture.

↗ Visit Site
Explanation

For The Whole Sky on A Page I utilized the Canva website building platform to organize and manage resources relating to the history of astronomical writing. It presents users with several galleries that take the user through the earliest recorded texts on astronomy to Copernicus's discovery of the heliocentric nature of our solar system. The webpage service manages images, text, and metadata.

Outcome 3c

Evaluate resources or tools that manage and facilitate access to information.

Artifact

Book Conservation and Binding

An object examination report and treatment proposal for Poetry Loves Poetry: An Anthology of Los Angeles Poets (1985). The treatment plan proposes a transition from softcover to hardcover with a cover inset preserving the original covers, and a double fan adhesive bind for the text block.

LIS 804  ·  Object Examination & Treatment Proposal  ·  Amanda Masis ↓ Download PDF
Explanation

For Outcome 3c I chose my book repair treatment plan. I wanted to show how conservation is an important part in facilitating information access. In this project I evaluate what the best methods are for conserving this particular book, engaging with theories and practices I learned in my LIS 804 course.
