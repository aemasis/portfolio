Goal 3

Navigate, Curate & Create

Navigate, curate and create information across the spectrum of human records.

Outcome 3a

Design resources or tools that facilitate access to information.

Artifact

A Guide to Knitting

A website developed for INF 253, Intro to Web Design. The site is designed to be a resource for people who are just getting started with knitting and want to know more about its historical and cultural significance.

Outcome 3b

Utilize resources or tools to manage information.

Artifact

The Whole Sky On A Page

A website built for the History of the Book course. The website is thematized around a mock exhibition on the history of astronomy told from the perspective of material book culture. The books range from a Late Babylonian (1000–500 BC) tablet to a first edition of Copernicus' De revolutionibus orbium coelestium from 1543.

↗ Visit Site
Outcome 3c

Evaluate resources or tools that manage and facilitate access to information.

Artifact

Textile History

A subject guide created for LIS 702 on facilitating user experience. This webpage acts as an interactive subject guide providing users with eight books and four websites to begin exploring textile and material history.
