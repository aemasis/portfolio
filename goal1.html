Professional Identity

Develop a professional identity and philosophy within the library and information professions.

Outcome 1a

Describe the evolving nature of the core values and ethics within diverse information environments.

Artifact

Explanation

I chose this paper for Outcome 1a because the Affective Archiving paper explores how concepts from affect theory can be borrowed to better understand the impact that the archive has on its users and subjects. It is through this research that I have described how archival theory and practice have evolved to incorporate the idea of affect. This incorporation has in turn shaped the ethics and core values of the practice.

Outcome 1b

Demonstrate how a philosophy, theory, model, and/or major perspective of the library and information profession guides practice in diverse settings.

Artifact

Placemaking and the Library

How can we borrow the concept of placemaking from urban studies and incorporate it into the library?

PDF  ·  LIS 707 ↓ Download PDF
Explanation

The Placemaking and the Library paper was chosen to demonstrate Outcome 1b because it utilizes theories of placemaking to better understand how the notion of "place" can be used to better serve library patrons. Placemaking is the idea that people create the spaces they inhabit through usage patterns and embedding their cultural signifiers within a space. The paper aims to show how understanding how communities produce and inhabit space can inform how library workers approach their roles to produce better programming and service. Placemaking can be a useful framework for understanding that each community has its own set of diverse needs and that there are actionable steps library workers can take to fulfill these needs.
